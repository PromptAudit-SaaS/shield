# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique the work rather than the person. Welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Keep discussions relevant and substantiate technical claims.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, impersonation and spam are not acceptable. Never publish credentials, personal records or confidential source. Use fictional reproductions and the private route in SECURITY.md for vulnerabilities.

These rules apply to this project's issues, discussions, pull requests and other project-managed community spaces. Report conduct concerns privately to info@promptaudit-vault.ch with minimal relevant information; do not post private evidence publicly.

Maintainers may request edits, remove inappropriate content, lock conversations or restrict participation, proportionately to the circumstances. Reports are reviewed with discretion; no absolute confidentiality, response-time or enforcement guarantee is promised. This is a project-specific policy, not a certification or a legal determination.
